Services/Accounting & Financial

Accounting & financial services

A business partner who integrates seamlessly into your operations — trustworthy, skilled accountants with none of the overhead of full-time employees.

Far more than recording transactions

Our team offers a complete range of accounting and bookkeeping services. We prepare interim financial statements and work with you to develop and manage operating budgets, cash-flow forecasts, and the projections you need to secure financing and keep shareholders informed.

We’ve worked with startups and established organizations alike, often designing or redesigning a chart of accounts to reflect the specific operational and transactional structure of the business. Our team works in QuickBooks, Sage 50, and Sage Intacct.

Specialty

Accounting for federal government contractors

Government contracting has unique requirements. We help contractors set up compliant accounting systems from the start — avoiding difficulties, lost profit, or lost contracts.

Indirect rate calculations
Contract cost & pricing proposals
Accounting system setup & implementation
Accounting policies & procedures
Incurred cost statements to DCAA
DCAA audit assistance
Provisional rate budgets & forward pricing
