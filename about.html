Owner

Karen Dolan

Karen has spent more than 30 years advising businesses on accounting, budgeting, payroll, benefits, and year-end preparation, for small and medium-sized companies in five states. She holds a degree in Business Administration from the University of Connecticut, with graduate coursework in accounting, marketing, finance, business law, nonprofit management, and federal contracting.

She is certified through the American Payroll Association and the Society for Human Resource Management, previously held management roles at Coleco Industries and Moore McCormack Resources, and serves on nonprofit boards in her community.
